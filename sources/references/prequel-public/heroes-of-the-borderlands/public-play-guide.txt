D&D STARTER SET: HEROES OF THE 
BORDERLANDS PUBLIC PLAY GUIDANCE 
The D&D Starter Set: Heroes of the Borderlands is the 
newest introductory D&D set, and uses many 
components to create an attractive, easy, and fun 
way for prospective players to learn how to play 
D&D. What follows is guidance for conventions, 
game stores, and other public play experiences. 
ORGANIZER NOTES 
Unlike other starter products, Heroes of the 
Borderlands has a strict maximum of four players 
plus one Dungeon Master. It should not be 
attempted to run sessions of play with more than 
four players, as it requires multiple box sets and 
doubling up on character classes is not optimal.  
 Everything is included to play in the box – there is 
no need to reference anything outside of the 
components inside the box.  
DUNGEON MASTERING 
Heroes of the Borderlands is easy to pick up and 
Dungeon Master. If you as the organizer have 
scheduled a longer learn-to-play session, you may 
decide to have the players pick a Dungeon Master 
from their group. If you are running demos only (see 
below), this is not recommended.  
 For both the demo and the two-hour session 
below, you won’t need the Keep on the 
Borderlands Booklet.  
DEMO EXPERIENCE 
To run a short, approximately 20-minute demo of 
D&D, set up as follows: 
• From the Wilderness Booklet, choose 
Region 1: Trail and set up the Bandit 
Ambush encounter. Remove one Bandit.  
• Set up each player board (Cleric, Fighter, 
Rogue, Wizard) on the level 1 side in a static 
location at the table. Choose a species and 
background for each. Place the remainder of 
the cards needed for each board near the 
board.  
• Set out the remaining components, such as 
the Trail map, monster cards, and the 
tokens for the players and bandits.  
• When players arrive, each one chooses a 
seat. When they leave, reset the player 
boards for the next group.  
• Players can share dice from the boxed set.  
• Jump into the action, as you have limited 
time. You can use the script below to begin.  
Hi everyone. I’m going to run you through a quick 
demo on how to play D&D. I’m the Dungeon Master, so 
it’s my job to tell you what’s happening in the world 
around you and rolling any dice for the foes. You’re 
playing the heroes!  
 Just about anything you can think of to try will 
require some combination of skill, natural ability, and 
luck to pull off. And the D&D game represents that by 
having you roll one of these 20-sided dice and add a 
number from your sheet. Let’s begin! 
TWO HOUR LEARN-TO-PLAY 
For a longer, more involved session lasting 90 
minutes to two hours, set up as follows: 
• From the Caves of Chaos Booklet, choose 
Cave B: Mephit Lair.  
• Set out the Mephit Lair map and prepare the 
monster cards and tokens you will need. 
Keep the rest of the components and player 
boards nearby, but do not set them up yet.  
• When the players arrive, each of them 
selects a character to play, as well as their 
species and background. Give them all of 
their components needed for play. You can 
use the Quick Start Guide to help you set 
things up this way. 
• If you decide to have one player take on the 
role of Dungeon Master, you’ll want to have 
a facilitator nearby to help with questions. 
They use the Quick Start Guide to set up. 
• Players can share dice from the boxed set.  
• If needed, you may modify the demo script 
to introduce the game (see read-aloud text 
above).  
• When the players complete the session, 
leave the map, monster cards and 
associated tokens out, but you may put the 
rest of the components back in the box or in 
a neat pile nearby for another group.  
AFTER THE SESSION 
Make sure to let the players know about the new 
Player’s Handbook as well as D&D Beyond! If you 
have other play experiences at your event that are 
good for new players, tell them how they can 
participate.
