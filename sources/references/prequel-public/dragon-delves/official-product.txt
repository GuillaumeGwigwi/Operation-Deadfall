Meet the dragons of D&D in Dragon Delves, a collection of ten dragon-themed adventures for characters level 1 through 12. Featuring artwork rendered in ten unique styles, these ready-to-play adventures include streamlined prep and quick start guides for Dungeon Masters. Delve into mysterious dungeons, explore captivating fantasy locations, and come face to face with all the chromatic and metallic dragons, each with unique temperaments, alignments, and magic.
Contents:
10 dragon-themed adventures for characters level 1 through 12, each set in a different fantasy location with a distinctive dragon lair
Unique art styles for each adventure from 10 illustrators and 7 cartographers
3 of the 10 adventures can be run with a solo player
Gallery of dragon art from D&D's history that precedes each of the 10 adventures
Maps for each adventure, from a stolen pirate base to city ruins
Quick start guides for each adventure that summarize key plot points, key NPCs, and other essential details for Dungeon Masters to get set up quickly
Guidance on how to run each adventure individually or as part of a campaign
Stickers and 22 pre-populated maps for use on D&D Beyond with Maps, the 2D VTT
