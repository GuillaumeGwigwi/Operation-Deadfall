# History of Bronze Dragons

AUJIR DARASTRIX

At first glance, the bronze dragon art in the 2025 [Monster Manual](book.html#xmm) bears little resemblance to the dragon's earliest depictions. But close examination reveals that the small horns and frills on the dragon's head have been there every step of the way.
