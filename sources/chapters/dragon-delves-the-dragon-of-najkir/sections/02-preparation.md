# Preparation

Before running the adventure, prepare as follows:

* Step 1. If you're running it for a single character, read the ["Running for One Character](#drde-tdon,1,running%20for%20one%20character,0)" section.
* Step 2. Read "[Adventure Background](#drde-tdon,1,adventure%20background,0)" and "[The Monks](#drde-tdon,1,the%20monks,0)" for more context. Familiarize yourself with the Key NPCs table, which lists individuals the characters will interact with throughout the adventure.
* Step 3. Familiarize yourself with the Ship map in the [Dungeon Master's Guide](book.html#xdmg) or on D&D Beyond and the game statistics for [Sailing Ships](vehicles.html#sailing%20ship_gos) and [Rowboats](vehicles.html#rowboat_gos) in the [Player's Handbook](book.html#xphb) or on D&D Beyond.
* Step 4. Bookmark the following stat blocks in the [Monster Manual](book.html#xmm) or on D&D Beyond:

* [Assassin](bestiary.html#assassin_xmm)
* [Bronze Dragon, Young](bestiary.html#young%20bronze%20dragon_xmm)
* [Commoner](bestiary.html#commoner_xmm)
* [Doppelganger](bestiary.html#doppelganger_xmm)
* [Goat](bestiary.html#goat_xmm)
* [Noble](bestiary.html#noble_xmm)
* [Oni](bestiary.html#oni_xmm)
* [Priest](bestiary.html#priest_xmm)
* [Scout](bestiary.html#scout_xmm)

Young Bronze Dragon (Info)

[+]

|  |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Many young bronze dragons become experts in a type of problem, like driving off pirates or protecting communities from storms. Young bronze dragons collect friends with varied expertise, cultivating a community of experts they can rely on.   ---   Bronze Dragons *Dragons of Potential and Preservation*   * Habitat: [Coastal](bestiary.html#blankhash,flstenvironment:coastal=1) * Treasure: [Implements](tables.html#random%20magic%20items%20-%20implements_xdmg)   Where bronze dragons dwell, wonders flourish. Imaginative yet mindful, these metallic dragons work toward greatness and help others achieve all they can. They strive to preserve innovations, from the works of past civilizations to new discoveries, and they share such works widely. When dealing with shorter-lived beings, bronze dragons prefer to win them over through conversation and cultivation, but they don't shy from battle when villains keep others from achieving their potential.  Bronze dragons enjoy the power and endless possibilities of the sea, and they often make their lairs in places of natural beauty or communities they wish to preserve. Within their dwellings, bronze dragons hoard things they believe will be useful one day. They salvage treasure lost to the sea, reclaiming wealth or sunken ships.  Bronze Dragon Lairs Bronze dragons usually make their homes near or under the sea. | | | | | |
