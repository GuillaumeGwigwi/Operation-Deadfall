#### Lighting

Sconces and braziers with [Continual Flame](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619065-continual-flame) spells cast on them illuminate the sanctum with green-tinted [Dim Light](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#DimLight). The exception is [area Z13](../chapter.md#Z13HallOfCorruptedLava), where the green lava’s glow creates eerie [Bright Light](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#BrightLight).
