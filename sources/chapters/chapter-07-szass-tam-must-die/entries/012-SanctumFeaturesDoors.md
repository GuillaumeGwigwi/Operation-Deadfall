#### Doors

The sanctum’s doors are made of stone. All are unlocked except the double door to the hall of corrupted lava ([area Z13](../chapter.md#Z13HallOfCorruptedLava)), which is locked with an [Arcane Lock](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618868-arcane-lock) spell. (See “Doors” in the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#Doors).)
