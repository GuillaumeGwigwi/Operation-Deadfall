#### Ceilings

Ceilings in the sanctum are 40 feet high, with the exception of areas [Z13](../chapter.md#Z13HallOfCorruptedLava) and [Z14](../chapter.md#Z14RitualChamber). Ceilings in those areas are 100 feet high.
