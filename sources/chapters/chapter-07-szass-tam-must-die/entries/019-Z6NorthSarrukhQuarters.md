#### Z6: North Sarrukh Quarters

Several nests made from blankets lie to the north.

If the characters spend 10 minutes searching the northwest nest, they find Vartheeth’s journal, written in Draconic. It reveals the information she might mention in [area Z2](../chapter.md#Z2CommunalHall).
