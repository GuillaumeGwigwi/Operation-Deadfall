# J: Gnoll Lair

Evil gnolls—tall, shaggy fiends with hyenoid heads—have turned this abandoned shrine into their lair. The gnolls heard rumors that treasure is hidden somewhere in this cave, but they've been unable to find it due to an illusion placed by a long-dead mage. Vicious and hot-tempered, the gnolls have little patience for those who interrupt their search.

You can add monsters to the cave to make this scenario longer and more difficult. One or more [Bugbear Warriors](bestiary.html#bugbear%20warrior_xmm) might have formed a tenuous alliance with the gnolls. Conversely, you can reduce the number of [Gnoll Warriors](bestiary.html#gnoll%20warrior_xmm) to make the scenario easier and shorter.

## Getting Started

To begin, read the following boxed text aloud:

All is quiet outside the yawning mouth of this natural cave, at the back of which you can just make out a pair of featureless stone doors.

## Gnoll Lair Features

The cave has the following features:

* Ceilings. The cave's ceilings are 10 feet high.
* Doors. The doors to [area J2](#hotb,1,j2%3a%20junk%20pile,0) are made of stone.
* Environment. Past its entrance, the cave's natural rock gives way to crumbling stone blocks and flagstone tiles. Its walls are decorated with painted motifs of grinning demons and marching skeletons.
* Lighting. The cave is unlit, and its denizens rely on Darkvision to see. Area descriptions assume the characters have a light source or other means of seeing in [Darkness](variantrules.html#darkness_xphb).

### J1: Entrance

Set into the west wall of this natural cavern is a stone double door that is partly open. Bestial, high-pitched laughter echoes within.

The gnolls rigged the door with a makeshift alarm.

* Door Alarm. This simple alarm consists of armor scraps and rusty weapons balanced atop the partly open double door. Moving either door triggers the trap, the rules for which are as follows:
* Avoiding the Trap. A Medium or smaller creature can slip through the gap between the doors with a successful DC 13 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check; failing this check triggers the trap.
* Detect and Disable. As a [Search](actions.html#search_xphb) action, a character can examine the door and make a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check, detecting the trap on a successful check. Once detected, the trap can be disabled by a character who takes the [Utilize](actions.html#utilize_xphb) action and succeeds on a DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check; failing this check triggers the trap.
* Triggering the Trap. If the door is moved, the scraps clatter to the ground. The noise alerts all the gnolls in the cave, who cackle as they rush to dispatch intruders.

### J2: Junk Pile

The center of this chamber's floor is piled with loose junk—broken ceremonial implements, rusty candlestick holders, and rotten rags.

The gnolls piled objects here to sift through later.

* DM Tip! If you increased this cave's difficulty, one or more [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) [Bugbear Warriors](bestiary.html#bugbear%20warrior_xmm) might be here rummaging through the junk.

### J3: Old Chapel

This forlorn chapel is unfurnished, though a slightly depressed section of floor near the southeast wall suggests an altar or some other heavy object once rested there.

Two tall warriors with mangy hides and hocked knees move about the chamber slowly, their hyena-like heads sniffing its walls.

Two [Gnoll Warriors](bestiary.html#gnoll%20warrior_xmm) scour this chamber for secret doors. The [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) gnolls lunge at intruders in a frenzy. Combat in this chamber draws the attention of the gnolls in area J4, which quickly join the fray.

If the characters defeat all but one gnoll, the last of them surrenders. As an [Influence](actions.html#influence_xphb) action, a character can try to cow the gnoll into submission and make a DC 13 Charisma (Intimidation) check. On a successful check, the gnoll's attitude improves to [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb), and the gnoll reveals that its pack was searching for "a prize hidden in the mouth of a laughing fiend." On a failed check, the gnoll flees without divulging any information.

### J4: Shrine of the Laughing Devil

A stone dais spans much of the south wall of this eerie shrine. Above the dais, a mosaic of black and red tiles depicts the face of a laughing devil whose forked tongue curls down to the dais.

Unless drawn elsewhere, two [Gnoll Warriors](bestiary.html#gnoll%20warrior_xmm) pace about this chamber, frustrated in their fruitless search for treasure. The [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) gnolls behave similarly to the pair in area J3; combat in this chamber draws those gnolls here.

Illusory Wall. The laughing devil face is an illusory wall; it looks real to the eye but has no substance. A creature that touches the illusory wall discovers its true nature and can pass right through it.

As a [Study](actions.html#study_xphb) action, a character can make a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check to discern the wall's true nature. On a successful check, the character recognizes the wall is a magical illusion.

### J5: Hidden Crypt

The devil's face ripples to reveal a dusty crypt. Ancient bones and rubble litter the tiled floor. An austere sarcophagus stands in each corner, and an ornate chest rests in the center of the room.

Long ago, an evil mage reanimated the corpses of four explorers and commanded them to guard this crypt's treasure for all eternity. Entombed here are four [Zombies](bestiary.html#zombie_xmm), one in each sarcophagus.

If the characters touch any of the sarcophagi or the chest, the [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) [zombies](bestiary.html#zombie_xmm) push the lids off their containers and start combat, seeking to rend intruders limb from limb.

Treasure. Among the debris is the skeleton of a human thief. The thief's purse contains 15 GP and a large gemstone (worth 100 GP).

The chest is unlocked. It contains one magic item; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item.
