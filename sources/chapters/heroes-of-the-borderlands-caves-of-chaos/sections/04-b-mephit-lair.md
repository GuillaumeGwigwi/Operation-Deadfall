# B: Mephit Lair

Sturdy fortifications and clean water make this abandoned subterranean outpost an appealing den for thieves and monsters. Before the prior inhabitants left, they stored some enticing treasure here, guarded by a monstrous shape-shifter called a mimic. Since then, a trio of impish elementals and kuo-toa fanatics have made the cave their home.

You can add monsters, such as [Giant Centipedes](bestiary.html#giant%20centipede_xmm), to the cave to make this scenario longer and more difficult, or you can remove the [Kuo-Toa Marauders](bestiary.html#kuo-toa%20marauder_hotb) or one of the [Steam Mephits](bestiary.html#steam%20mephit_xmm) to make it easier and shorter.

## Getting Started

To begin, read the following boxed text aloud:

Walls of mortared stone and cracked flagstone tiles form the interior of this cave, creating the impression of a subterranean fortress. Water trickles steadily somewhere in the cave.

Water from the broken fountain in [area B7](#hotb,1,b7%3a%20broken%20fountain,0) floods a chamber to the south ([area B5](#hotb,1,b5%3a%20flooded%20room,0)). The dribbling fountain can be heard throughout the cave.

## Mephit Lair Features

The cave has the following features:

* Ceilings. The cave's ceilings are 10 feet high.
* Door. The door to [area B8](#hotb,1,b8%3a%20vault,0) is made from reinforced wood; it's closed but unlocked.
* Environment. The cave's walls are made of smooth, mortared stones with patches of unworked rock.
* Lighting. The cave is unlit, and its denizens rely on Darkvision to see. Area descriptions assume the characters have a light source or other means of seeing in [Darkness](variantrules.html#darkness_xphb).

### B1: Entrance

An arrow slit interrupts the wall at this junction.

The arrow slit is linked to area B2. If the characters enter the cave carrying a source of [Bright Light](variantrules.html#bright%20light_xphb) or talking loudly, the slit begins to vent harmless steam as the [Steam Mephit](bestiary.html#steam%20mephit_xmm) in that area approaches the opening and observes them. The slit provides the mephit with [Three-Quarters Cover](tables.html#cover_xphb).

### B2: Balistraria

Steam fills this stone alcove, which looks out to the cave's entrance through a thin vertical slit.

Defenders can watch the cave entrance from this alcove. A [Steam Mephit](bestiary.html#steam%20mephit_xmm) flits about the area. It ostensibly watches for intruders coming from area B1, but it is bored and barely fulfilling its duties. The mephit is [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) but defends itself in combat.

If the mephit sees the characters enter the cave, it peers at them through the arrow slit and whistles like a teakettle to entice them to approach. If a character comes within 5 feet of the slit, the mephit exhales a 15-foot [Cone](variantrules.html#cone%20%5barea%20of%20effect%5d_xphb) of harmless steam and snickers at the character's reaction.

If the characters approach from the other side of the alcove, the mephit feigns innocence. It then tries to lure them to the old barrack ([area B6](#hotb,1,b6%3a%20old%20barrack,0)) to be ambushed by the kuo-toa that lurk there.

### B3: Prison Cells

Four cold cells line one wall of this chamber. Floor-to-ceiling iron bars partition the cells, which are unlocked and devoid of valuables. Two of the cells contain unidentifiable skeletal remains draped in moth-eaten rags.

### B4: Old Armory

Cobwebbed weapon racks and rusty suits of armor lie scattered about this armory. None of the items is salvageable.

### B5: Flooded Room

Water trickles into this spacious chamber from the north, forming a shallow pool. A small, winged creature made of hissing steam zips above the water in pursuit of a tiny croaking frog.

Warm spring water streams into this room from [area B7](#hotb,1,b7%3a%20broken%20fountain,0), forming a 1-foot-deep pool here. The pool is [Difficult Terrain](variantrules.html#difficult%20terrain_xphb).

A [Steam Mephit](bestiary.html#steam%20mephit_xmm) toys with a panicked frog in the water. The mephit is initially [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward the characters and remains that way unless they try to spoil its fun, which provokes the hot-tempered elemental to start combat. If a fight breaks out, the mephits in [areas B2](#hotb,1,b2%3a%20balistraria,0) and B7 quickly join the fray.

### B6: Old Barrack

Eric Belisle

Ruined cots are heaped near the corners of this chamber, which features a natural ceiling. Two slimy humanoids with fishlike heads use spears to carve strange symbols on the cave walls.

Two [Kuo-Toa Marauders](bestiary.html#kuo-toa%20marauder_hotb) scrawl rupestrian gibberish on the walls of this run-down barrack. The [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) kuo-toa fight the characters on sight, hoping to capture them alive and hold them in the prison cells ([area B3](#hotb,1,b3%3a%20prison%20cells,0)). The room is otherwise empty.

### B7: Broken Fountain

A badly broken stone fountain burbles in an alcove at the upper end of this sloped hallway.

Warm water bubbles from an underground spring into this ruined fountain. It flows south and pools in [area B5](#hotb,1,b5%3a%20flooded%20room,0).

Unless it was drawn elsewhere, a [Steam Mephit](bestiary.html#steam%20mephit_xmm) lurks near the fountain, quietly mesmerized by the fountain's burbling. The mephit behaves similarly to the one in area B5, starting combat with the characters if disturbed. If a fight breaks out, the mephits in [areas B2](#hotb,1,b2%3a%20balistraria,0) and B5 join the fray.

### B8: Vault

Dust coats this plundered vault, which is mostly empty. Two chests rest along the east wall.

One of the chests is fake—a hungry [Mimic](bestiary.html#mimic_xmm) has altered its shape to resemble the mundane container beside it. The mimic ambushes any creature that comes within 5 feet of it. It fights to the death.
