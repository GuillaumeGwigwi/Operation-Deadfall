# G: Shunned Caves

An owlbear—a frightening cross between a bear and a giant owl—lurks in this cave. Most other denizens of the valley give the cave a wide berth.

This cave is connected to [cave K](#hotb,1,k%3a%20shrine%20of%20evil%20chaos,0), a hotbed of activity for the Cult of Chaos, whose members venerate powers of disorder and destruction. The cult mainly operates in the adjacent cave, but as its ranks swell, cultists increasingly frequent this one. The owlbear is unaware of the cultists' presence.

You can add monsters, such as [Zombies](bestiary.html#zombie_xmm), to the cave to make this scenario longer and more difficult, or you can reduce the number of [Cultists](bestiary.html#cultist_xmm) or [Gray Ooze Globs](bestiary.html#gray%20ooze%20glob_hotb) to make it easier and shorter.

## Getting Started

This cave is connected to [cave K](#hotb,1,k%3a%20shrine%20of%20evil%20chaos,0) via the stairs south of [area G6](#hotb,1,g6%3a%20cult%20barrack,0) (see [map G](#hotb,1,undefined,0)). If the characters approach from outside the cave, read the following boxed text aloud to begin:

The stench of rot and wet stone issues from this natural cave. An unsettling screech and the sound of thick feathers ruffling echo within.

The owlbear is moving restlessly in its den ([area G5](#hotb,1,g5%3a%20owlbear%20den,0)).

## Shunned Cave Features

The cave has the following features:

* Ceilings. The cave's ceilings are 15 feet high.
* Door. The stone door to [area G6](#hotb,1,g6%3a%20cult%20barrack,0) is ajar.
* Environment. The eastern half of this cave ([areas G1](#hotb,1,g1%3a%20entrance,0)–[G5](#hotb,1,g5%3a%20owlbear%20den,0)) has naturally formed spaces with wet walls and damp dirt floors. The western half of the cave ([areas G6](#hotb,1,g6%3a%20cult%20barrack,0)–[G7](#hotb,1,g7%3a%20crypt,0)) has walls made of cut stone blocks and floors made of cracked tiles.
* Lighting. Unless noted otherwise, the cave is unlit. Its denizens use Darkvision or other means to see. Area descriptions assume the characters have a light source or other means of seeing in [Darkness](variantrules.html#darkness_xphb).

### G1: Entrance

Gnawed bones litter the ground near the cave mouth, and the stench of blood hangs in the air. The bones belong to the owlbear's victims, including animals, explorers, and other monsters. The bones bear indentations from the owlbear's toothed beak.

### G2: Shallow Pool

[![](img/adventure/HotB/040-01-020.gray-ooze-glob-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/040-01-020.gray-ooze-glob-c.webp "Art credit: Wade Acuff")

Wade Acuff

Moisture drips from the ceiling of this damp cave, forming a shallow pool. Something glitters in the murky water.

Two [Gray Ooze Globs](bestiary.html#gray%20ooze%20glob_hotb) lurk in the pool, which is 2 feet deep and [Difficult Terrain](variantrules.html#difficult%20terrain_xphb). The [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) oozes fight any creature that comes within 5 feet of the water.

Treasure. The pool contains a jewel-encrusted goblet (worth 100 GP) and one magic item; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item.

### G3: Tight Passage

A narrow crack spans the cave wall here. The opening is only big enough for a Medium or smaller creature to fit through. Inside, the mangled corpse of a human explorer slumps against a stalagmite.

The owlbear in [area G5](#hotb,1,g5%3a%20owlbear%20den,0) can't fit through the crack. If a character seeks refuge here, the owlbear loses interest and returns to its den.

### G4: Smelly Refuse

This cave contains heaps of food scraps, rotting carcasses, and a scattering of halfling-size bird pellets composed of fur and regurgitated bones.

### G5: Owlbear Den

[![](img/adventure/HotB/041-01-022.owlbear-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/041-01-022.owlbear-c.webp "Art credit: Zuzanna Wuzyk")

Zuzanna Wuzyk

A monstrous creature with a thick, shaggy hide and a feathered, owllike head paces aggressively in this den between nests formed from damp leaves and bloodstained branches.

A territorial [Owlbear](bestiary.html#owlbear_xmm) lairs in this den. The [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) owlbear fights all other creatures on sight.

Ledge. At the chamber's south end, a ledge 10 feet above the floor marks the entrance to a tunnel to areas G6 and [G7](#hotb,1,g7%3a%20crypt,0). To climb the cave's slippery walls to reach the ledge, a character must succeed on a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check. The owlbear doesn't pursue characters beyond the ledge.

### G6: Cult Barrack

These cult quarters contain beds, a table, and several chairs. Tapestries on the walls depict evil creatures and obscene rituals. Four humans in hooded red robes recline on the beds.

[Torches](items.html#torch_xphb) in wall sconces fill this room with [Bright Light](variantrules.html#bright%20light_xphb). Four [Cultists](bestiary.html#cultist_xmm) relax in these quarters for the Cult of Chaos. They are nonchalantly discussing the finer points of mayhem, torture, and other cruelties.

The cultists are [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward intruders not garbed in their cult's attire and eagerly inflict violence on nonbelievers. As an [Influence](actions.html#influence_xphb) action, a character can try to de-escalate the situation and make one of the following ability checks (with [Advantage](variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) if the character is dressed like a member of the Cult of Chaos). Consider these examples:

**DC 13 Charisma (Deception).** The character deceives the cultists with a lie, such as pretending to be a fellow cultist or an aspiring member.

**DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion).** The character convinces the cultists that it is in both parties' interests not to fight.

On a successful check, the cultists' attitudes improve to [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb). Otherwise, the cultists fight.

Treasure. Footlockers under four of the beds contain the cultists' belongings. Each contains 5 GP, a few worthless personal effects, and a [Cultist's Robe](items.html#cultist's%20robe_hotb), which the characters can also take from cultists defeated here.

### G7: Crypt

Damp stairs descend to this musty crypt. A closed stone sarcophagus sits at the north end.

A [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) [Wight Lifedrinker](bestiary.html#wight%20lifedrinker_hotb) rests in the sarcophagus. The monster was once a cult leader who was so wicked in life that when he died, he rose as a cruel undead warrior. As an action, a character can pry open the lid of the sarcophagus, at which point the wight springs forth and fights.

Helge C. Balzer

Treasure. The sarcophagus holds one magic item; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item.
