# F: Hobgoblin Lair

Hobgoblins quartered in this cave recently suffered heavy losses, including their former captain. Only four hobgoblins remain. They've fortified their outpost and drafted a voracious ooze to guard it.

You can add monsters, such as allied [Goblin Warriors](bestiary.html#goblin%20warrior_xmm), to the cave to make this scenario longer and more difficult, or you can remove some monsters to make it easier and shorter. One or two [Hobgoblin Warriors](bestiary.html#hobgoblin%20warrior_xmm) might be out on a patrol elsewhere.

## Getting Started

This cave is connected to [cave D](#hotb,1,d%3a%20goblin%20lair,0) via the hall east of [area F2](#hotb,1,f2%3a%20cube%20route,0) (see [map F](#hotb,1,undefined,0)). If the characters approach from outside the cave, read the following boxed text aloud to begin:

A stout, ironbound door seals this cave mouth. Bleached skulls line niches outside the entrance. A bloodstained sign on the door reads, "Come in! We'd like to have you for dinner!"

The hobgoblins don't eat people, but they use the sign's gruesome implication to scare intruders.

## Hobgoblin Lair Features

The cave has the following features:

* Ceilings. The cave's ceilings are 10 feet high.
* Doors. The cave's doors are made of oak planks bound with iron. They are unlocked.
* Environment. The cave is carved from stone. It is scrupulously clean. The hobgoblins keep their areas tidy, and the gelatinous cube in [area F2](#hotb,1,f2%3a%20cube%20route,0) sweeps up anything in its path.
* Lighting. The cave is unlit, and its denizens rely on Darkvision or other means to see. Area descriptions assume the characters have a light source or other means of seeing in [Darkness](variantrules.html#darkness_xphb).

### F1: Entrance

An armored goblinoid warrior with red-orange skin and a bow stands behind a rickety barricade of interlocking spears. "Halt!" she commands. "State your business or face the wrath of our battalion—thirty hobgoblins strong."

A [Hobgoblin Warrior](bestiary.html#hobgoblin%20warrior_xmm) stands guard behind a pair of spear barricades (see the "[Spear Barricade](#hotb,1,spear%20barricade,0)" sidebar). The sentry is [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward intruders and tries to delay them through interrogation until the gelatinous cube in [area F2](#hotb,1,f2%3a%20cube%20route,0) arrives here to dispatch them.

The sentry is lying about how many hobgoblins remain. Weakened by their recent loss, the hobgoblins fear marauders and try to present a strong front. A character who doubts the sentry can take the [Search](actions.html#search_xphb) action and make a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check; a successful check discerns the lie.

As an [Influence](actions.html#influence_xphb) action, a character can try to pacify the sentry and make a DC 14 Charisma (Persuasion) check. A character who saw through her previous lie has Advantage on this check. On a successful check, the sentry's attitude improves to [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb), and she stands down; she warns the characters about the gelatinous cube's impending arrival but doesn't let them past the barricades. On a failed check, the sentry commands the characters to leave at once or be destroyed.

Fighting the Hobgoblins. In combat, the sentry remains behind the barricades. If the characters breach either barricade, the sentry uses her next action to blow a war horn, alerting the other hobgoblins in the cave. She then retreats to the barrack ([area F4](#hotb,1,f4%3a%20barrack,0)), where she and the rest of the hobgoblins make their last stand.

#### Spear Barricade

A spear barricade is a bristly cube of interlocking spears. Each spear barricade has these rules.

Difficult Terrain. The barricade is [Difficult Terrain](variantrules.html#difficult%20terrain_xphb). A creature that moves through the barricade takes 1d6 Piercing damage.

Provides Half Cover. A creature behind the barricade has [Half Cover](tables.html#cover_xphb).

Breaking the Barricade. The barricade has AC 15, HP 4, and Immunity to Poison and Psychic damage. If reduced to 0 Hit Points, the barricade is destroyed.

### F2: Cube Route

[![](img/adventure/HotB/036-01-019.jelly-cube-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/036-01-019.jelly-cube-c.webp "Art credit: Olivier Bernard")

Olivier Bernard

This smooth tunnel forms a rectangular circuit.

A hungry [Gelatinous Cube](bestiary.html#gelatinous%20cube_xmm) slowly loops counterclockwise through this part of the cave. It takes about 1 minute for it to complete a lap. The cube occasionally meanders down the eastern tunnel toward [cave D](#hotb,1,d%3a%20goblin%20lair,0) and back again, but it never crosses the spear barricades (see the "[Spear Barricade](#hotb,1,spear%20barricade,0)" sidebar).

The cube is [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward all other creatures and fights them on detection. Clever characters might be able to lure the cube out of the cave through the entrance to area F1, shutting the door behind them.

### F3: Mess Hall

The hobgoblins dine in this mess hall. They erected a pair of spear barricades (see the "[Spear Barricade](#hotb,1,spear%20barricade,0)" sidebar) to keep the gelatinous cube in area F2 out. The room contains nothing of value to the characters.

### F4: Barrack

This chamber contains a dozen stiff-looking cots, but only four have seen recent use. A large iron strongbox sits against the east wall. A hobgoblin warrior, sword in hand, stands before it.

The surviving hobgoblins lodge in this once-full barrack. A lone [Hobgoblin Warrior](bestiary.html#hobgoblin%20warrior_xmm) fiercely defends what few valuables remain. He starts combat with the characters on sight and fights to the death.

If the hobgoblin sentry sounded the alarm in [area F1](#hotb,1,f1%3a%20entrance,0), the two [Hobgoblin Warriors](bestiary.html#hobgoblin%20warrior_xmm) from area F5 are also here, ready to intercept the characters.

Treasure. The chest includes stolen goods: fine furs worth 75 GP, a pouch containing 20 GP, and one magic item; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item.

### F5: Training Hall

This training hall holds bare weapon racks and four straw-stuffed targets painted with bullseyes.

Two hobgoblin archers fire at the targets.

Unless they were called elsewhere, two [Hobgoblin Warriors](bestiary.html#hobgoblin%20warrior_xmm) train in this hall, which was stripped of its weapons during a raid. The hobgoblins are [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward intruders and fight them on sight.
