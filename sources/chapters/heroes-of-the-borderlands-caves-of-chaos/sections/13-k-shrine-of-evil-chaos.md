# K: Shrine of Evil Chaos

The Cult of Chaos performs evil rites in this cave, subtly drawing monsters to inhabit the Caves of Chaos and bring strife to the Borderlands. The current cult leader is Ivlis, the keep's former priest. Her cruel assistant, Narthus, is always here, and other cultists dwell close by (see [cave G](#hotb,1,g%3a%20shunned%20caves,0)). Undead guardians defend the shrine and the Chaos Bell—the source of the cult's foul magic—at the shrine's heart.

You can add monsters, such as [Cultists](bestiary.html#cultist_xmm), to the cave to make this scenario longer and more difficult, or you can reduce the number of [Skeletons](bestiary.html#skeleton_xmm) or [Zombies](bestiary.html#zombie_xmm) to make the scenario easier and shorter.

## Getting Started

This cave is connected to [cave G](#hotb,1,g%3a%20shunned%20caves,0) via the stairs east of [area K5](#hotb,1,k5%3a%20altar%20of%20evil%20chaos,0) (see [map K](#hotb,1,undefined,0)). If the characters approach from outside the cave, read the following boxed text aloud to begin:

A foul draft issues from this cave. Red strata intertwine with bulging black veins running through the hewn rock. A sinister crimson light dances deep in the cave.

The stench issues from the rotting ogre in [area K2](#hotb,1,k2%3a%20guarded%20hallway,0).

## Shrine Features

The cave has the following features:

* Ceilings. The cave's ceilings are 20 feet high.
* Doors and Secret Doors. Doors throughout this cave are made of heavy stone. The secret doors to [area K8](#hotb,1,k8%3a%20secret%20%22treasure%22,0) blend in with their surroundings but are obvious from inside area K8. As a [Search](actions.html#search_xphb) action, a character can make a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check to examine the wall for a secret door, noticing it on a successful check.
* Environment. The cave instills a sense of unease in its visitors.
* Lighting. Unless otherwise noted, [Bright Light](variantrules.html#bright%20light_xphb) from red-flamed wall sconces fills the cave's areas.

### K1: Entrance

Shadows shroud this foreboding entrance.

### K2: Guarded Hallway

[![](img/adventure/HotB/062-01-029.ogre-zombie-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/062-01-029.ogre-zombie-c.webp "Art credit: Helge C. Balzer")

Helge C. Balzer

The stench of death fills this hallway. Flies buzz around a groaning, slack-jawed giant backlit by red-flamed wall sconces.

An [Ogre Zombie](bestiary.html#ogre%20zombie_xmm) conscripted by the cultists to guard their nefarious acts stands watch here, but the undead brute is easily fooled.

The ogre zombie lets members of the Cult of Chaos pass unscathed, but it is [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward entrants not garbed in the cult's attire and pummels them on sight. The creature is [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward anyone wearing a [Cultist's Robe](items.html#cultist's%20robe_hotb).

### K3: Chapel of Evil Chaos

Four pillars support the domed roof of this empty chapel, where cultists conduct foul ceremonies.

* DM Tip! If you increased this cave's difficulty, two or more [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) [Cultists](bestiary.html#cultist_xmm) might be in the midst of an unholy ceremony here.

### K4: Undead Barrack

Four [Zombies](bestiary.html#zombie_xmm) shamble restlessly in this bare chamber. They fight any non-Undead not garbed in the cult's attire but are [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward anyone wearing a [Cultist's Robe](items.html#cultist's%20robe_hotb).

### K5: Altar of Evil Chaos

[![](img/adventure/HotB/063-01-031.narthus-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/063-01-031.narthus-c.webp "Art credit: David Auden Nash")

David Auden Nash

Violet draperies curtain this chamber. Three stone altars stand before a dais topped with five chairs: four rickety seats made of bone flanking a high-backed throne. An ominous, black iron bell hangs from a stand near the middle of the room.

Piles of humanoid bones are arranged neatly near the north wall. A cultist flecks the bones with blood while intoning a demonic chant.

[Narthus](bestiary.html#narthus_hotb) chants evil litanies in this impious chamber. He is [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward the characters, even if they're garbed in cult attire; Narthus screens every new cultist and knows his fellow cult members well.

If the characters interrupt the ritual, Narthus decries them as blasphemers and starts combat. On his turn, Narthus tries to ring the Chaos Bell, causing the bones to animate (see the "Chaos Bell" section).

Chaos Bell. Hanging from the stand is the Chaos Bell, a profane relic whose toll can wake the dead.

As a Bonus Action, a creature within 5 feet of the Chaos Bell can ring it, causing the bone piles to knit together and arise as four Hostile [Skeletons](bestiary.html#skeleton_xmm). The tolling bell also summons the [Zombies](bestiary.html#zombie_xmm) in [area K4](#hotb,1,k4%3a%20undead%20barrack,0) to the fray. The skeletons and zombies obey Narthus's commands.

[![](img/adventure/HotB/064-01-030.skeleton-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/064-01-030.skeleton-c.webp "Art credit: Zoltan Boros")

Zoltan Boros

The Chaos Bell is all but indestructible. To destroy the bell and end its magic, the characters must submerge it in the [sacred spring](#hotb,3,sacred%20spring,0) (see the "[Wilderness](#hotb,3,wilderness,0)" section). If a character removes the bell from its stand, give that player the Chaos Bell.

### K6: Visitor's Chamber

This room has comfortable chairs and a low table. Cult leaders interview potential new recruits here.

### K7: Cult Leader's Room

This opulent chamber contains a large bed, desk, and wardrobe. A ghastly demon head is mounted on the wall. Kneeling before the demon is a human woman wearing bloodred robes and a spidery headdress.

[Ivlis](bestiary.html#ivlis_hotb), leader of the Cult of Chaos, lives here. Each day, she pays tribute to the severed demon head mounted on the wall, which she talks to when no one's around. Ivlis is [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward the characters.

If confronted, Ivlis first tries to tempt the party with promises of untold riches and power, claiming all things are possible through the Cult of Chaos. She urges the characters to submit to the cult and become Undead. If they refuse, she fights.

[![](img/adventure/HotB/065-01-032.ivlis-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/065-01-032.ivlis-c.webp "Art credit: Diana Cearley")

Diana Cearley

Fighting Ivlis. In combat, the sneering cult leader fights arrogantly and doesn't hold back. If reduced to 15 Hit Points or fewer, however, she tries to flee the cave through area K8 via the secret door. If Ivlis escapes, she attempts to revive the cult elsewhere.

Treasure. The demon head pivots to reveal a nook with 100 GP, a large red [garnet](items.html#garnet_xdmg) (worth 100 GP), and two magic items; roll twice on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the items.

### K8: Secret "Treasure"

This hidden room contains only a black iron chest. When a creature moves within 5 feet of the chest, it springs open and spills 300 GP across the floor.

Treasure. The coins are cheap counterfeits intended to slow greedy pursuers. A cursory examination of the coins reveals them as fakes.
