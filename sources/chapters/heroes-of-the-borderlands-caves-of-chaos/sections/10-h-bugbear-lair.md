# H: Bugbear Lair

This cave recently came under the control of three belligerent bugbears and the goblin stooges they've cowed into their service. The bugbears are plotting an assault on the Keep on the Borderlands. They dream of destroying the keep and looting its riches.

You can add another [Bugbear Warrior](bestiary.html#bugbear%20warrior_xmm) to the cave to make this scenario longer and more difficult, or you can remove one or more of them to make it easier and shorter.

## Getting Started

This cave is connected to [cave I](#hotb,1,i%3a%20minotaur%20lair,0) via the the secret door in [area H5](#hotb,1,h5%3a%20hall,0) (see [map H](#hotb,1,undefined,0)). If the characters approach from outside the cave, read the following boxed text aloud to begin:

A tunnel of rough stone is tucked behind several bushes. Within, you hear two deep voices in a heated argument. The conversation is unintelligible at this range.

The voices belong to a pair of bugbears quarreling in the war room ([area H2](#hotb,1,h2%3a%20war%20room,0)).

## Bugbear Lair Features

The cave has the following features:

* Ceilings. The cave's ceilings are 10 feet high.
* Doors and Secret Doors. The doors in this cave are made of sturdy wood planks. The secret door in [area H5](#hotb,1,h5%3a%20hall,0) blends in with the surrounding wall. As a [Search](actions.html#search_xphb) action, a character can make a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check to scan that area for a secret door, noticing it on a successful check.
* Environment. The walls are made of rough-hewn stone in some places and neat-fitting stone blocks in others. Stone tiles cover the floor.
* Lighting. The cave is unlit, and its denizens rely on Darkvision to see. Area descriptions assume the characters have a light source or other means of seeing in [Darkness](variantrules.html#darkness_xphb).

### H1: Entrance

Just inside the cave, a tunnel branches east and west.

### H2: War Room

The door to this makeshift war room is ajar, allowing the characters to hear two gruff voices bickering inside. When the characters enter or peek inside, read or paraphrase the following text:

This room contains an old table and four upright logs. A poorly drawn map of the Borderlands covers the table. Two tall, furry goblinoids hunch over the map, arguing with each other.

Two [Bugbear Warriors](bestiary.html#bugbear%20warrior_xmm) squabble about how best to infiltrate the keep. One asserts that a stealthy approach would be best, while the other insists on a direct assault aided by a [local ogre mercenary](#hotb,1,e2%3a%20ogre%20mercenary's%20den,0) (see [cave E](#hotb,1,e%3a%20ogre%20lair,0)). The [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) bugbears fight the characters on sight and can't be bargained with.

### H3: Stairway

Stairs ascend north. At the top of the stairs is a heavy log the width of the stairway. Two goblins stand guard on a landing halfway up the stairs.

These two [Goblin Warriors](bestiary.html#goblin%20warrior_xmm) are [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward the characters. When the goblins detect an intruder to the south, roll Initiative.

On the goblins' turn, they attempt to scramble up the stairs and activate the rolling log trap (detailed below). The goblins then fight the characters.

Rolling Log Trap. This 10-foot-long log rolls down an incline, flattening creatures in its path.

As an action, a creature within 5 feet of the stationary log can push it down the stairs. The log tumbles down the incline in a straight line. Each creature in the log's path must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 2d6 Bludgeoning damage and has the [Prone](conditionsdiseases.html#prone_xphb) condition. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage only.

### H4: Trophy Room

The stuffed heads of deer, wolves, and other beasts are mounted on the walls of this room, as are a few humanoid heads with ghastly expressions. Skulls are piled in the northwest corner.

Among the trophies are a number of empty mounts equal to the characters in the party—a grim omen of what could happen if they're defeated in this cave.

Treasure. The skulls hide a purse containing 10 GP.

### H5: Hall

Musty animal furs serve as beds in this stark hall, where a furry brute berates two smaller, squeaky-voiced goblin subordinates.

A [Bugbear Warrior](bestiary.html#bugbear%20warrior_xmm)—the self-proclaimed boss of the cave—scolds two [Goblin Warriors](bestiary.html#goblin%20warrior_xmm) for sleeping on the job. When they notice the characters, the bugbear commands the goblins to fight and joins them in battle. The bugbear is [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward the party, while the goblins are [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb).

Swaying the Goblins. These fickle goblins, Gargle and Yams, were recently recruited and don't like being yelled at.

As an [Influence](actions.html#influence_xphb) action, a character can try to shake the goblins' loyalty and make one of the following checks (with [Advantage](variantrules.html#advantage_xphb) if the party already defeated the bugbear). Consider these examples:

**DC 10 Charisma (Deception).** The character tricks the goblins into believing the party now commands the cave.

**DC 11 Charisma (Intimidation).** The character scares the goblins into fearing for their lives.

**DC 10 Charisma (Persuasion).** The character convinces the goblins the party means them no harm.

On a successful check, the goblins' attitudes improve to [Friendly](variantrules.html#friendly%20%5battitude%5d_xphb). In exchange for a bribe of at least 2 GP, the goblins also help the characters defeat any remaining bugbears in the cave and tell the characters about the secret door in this area, though the goblins don't know what lies on the other side.

Treasure. Scattered among the furs on the floor are 25 GP and one magic item; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item.

### H6: Boss's Chamber

A heap of thick animal furs forms a soft bed in this room, where the bugbear boss in area H5 sleeps.

Treasure. Stashed in the furs are 25 GP and one magic item; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item.
