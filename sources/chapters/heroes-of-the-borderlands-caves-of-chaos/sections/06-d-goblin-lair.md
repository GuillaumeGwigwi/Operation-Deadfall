# D: Goblin Lair

A violent band of goblin warriors lives in this cave. It's the goblin boss's birthday, and she demands a celebration! Engrossed in the revelry, the goblins ignore their recent captive: a dwarf named Vinx.

You can add monsters to the cave to make this scenario longer and more difficult. The goblins might have invited [Hobgoblin Warriors](bestiary.html#hobgoblin%20warrior_xmm) from the adjacent cave ([cave F](#hotb,1,f%3a%20hobgoblin%20lair,0)) as party guests or bodyguards. To make the scenario easier and shorter, reduce the number of [Goblin Warriors](bestiary.html#goblin%20warrior_xmm).

## Getting Started

This cave is connected to [cave E](#hotb,1,e%3a%20ogre%20lair,0) via the secret door in [area D5](#hotb,1,d5%3a%20webbed%20cavern,0) (see [map D](#hotb,1,undefined,0)); characters can also enter from [cave F](#hotb,1,f%3a%20hobgoblin%20lair,0) through the hallway west of [area D4](#hotb,1,d4%3a%20boss's%20room,0). If the characters approach from outside the cave, read the following boxed text aloud to begin:

Torches illuminate this stinking cave. A horrendous racket of discordant voices echoes within. The voices appear to be singing—quite badly.

The "music" emanates from the boss's room (area D4), where off-key goblins commemorate their leader in song. The singing continues for 1 minute, then ends with a din of celebratory yips.

## Goblin Lair Features

The cave has the following features:

* Ceilings. The cave's ceilings are 10 feet high.
* Surroundings. The cave has walls of rough-hewn stone and a dirt floor. It stinks of old food and soiled clothes, which are strewn about the cave.
* Doors and Secret Door. Doors in the cave are made of rough wood. The secret door in [area D5](#hotb,1,d5%3a%20webbed%20cavern,0) blends in with the surrounding wall. As a [Search](actions.html#search_xphb) action, a character can make a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check to scan that area for a secret door, noticing it on a successful check.
* Lighting. [Torches](items.html#torch_xphb) in wall sconces throughout the cave fill it with [Bright Light](variantrules.html#bright%20light_xphb).

### D1: Entrance

The walls here are daubed with crude paintings of grinning goblins with pointy hats. Scrawled above the art are the words, "hoppee birthday!"

The goblins painted the celebratory drawings.

### D2: Dead-End Tunnel

This tunnel leads to a dead end with some old barrels. An exasperated dwarf is tied up beside them. "A little help here?" she asks as she squirms.

[![](img/adventure/HotB/024-01-013.vinx-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/024-01-013.vinx-c.webp "Art credit: Zoltan Boros")

Zoltan Boros

The captive is [Vinx](bestiary.html#vinx_hotb), a dwarf spelunker. Vinx came to this cave in search of a stolen family tapestry, but the goblins captured her. Vinx is [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward the characters and has the [Restrained](conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition while bound.

Vinx assures the characters that she's a capable explorer who can hold her own in a fight, but some quests can't be completed alone. If they help her recover the tapestry, she promises a hefty reward.

The barrels are mostly filled with crooked spears and old arrows that are unusable.

Freeing Vinx. As an action, a character can untie Vinx or cut her bonds (no check required). If freed, Vinx gratefully accompanies the characters as they explore the cave and fights alongside them until they all reemerge in the valley.

Treasure. Vinx stashed a magic item at the bottom of one of the barrels before she was captured; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item. If freed, Vinx discreetly fishes the item out of the barrel and keeps it on her person until the characters find her family's tapestry (see [area D4](#hotb,1,d4%3a%20boss's%20room,0)).

### D3: Common Room

Heaps of soiled bedding, tables, and stools are scattered around the room. Clumsily wrapped presents with tattered ribbons are piled on one table, along with a half-eaten cake that looks like it was dropped.

These goblin gifts include a bag of assorted teeth, a moist wad of thoughtfully pre-chewed jerky, and a foul-smelling mushroom. The cake appears professionally baked but poorly handled.

### D4: Boss's Room

[![](img/adventure/HotB/025-01-014.goblin-warrior-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/025-01-014.goblin-warrior-c.webp "Art credit: Brian Valeza")

Brian Valeza

In this rowdy chamber, a bossy goblin with a party hat and a kazoo sits on a wooden throne with a lopsided cushion. Goblin celebrants with cake-stained faces dance below.

A [Goblin Boss](bestiary.html#goblin%20boss_xmm) and four [Goblin Warriors](bestiary.html#goblin%20warrior_xmm) are here celebrating the boss's birthday. The goblins are initially [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) toward the characters.

When the goblins notice the characters, they stop dancing. Read or paraphrase the following text:

The goblin on the throne leaps to her feet and barks, "BREE-YARK! This is *my* party, and I don't remember inviting *you*!"

The goblins brandish their weapons.

[![](img/adventure/HotB/026-01-015.goblin-boss-c.webp)](img/adventure/HotB/026-01-015.goblin-boss-c.webp "Art credit: Brian Valeza")

Brian Valeza

**"Hoppee Birthday!"** As an [Influence](actions.html#influence_xphb) action, a character can try to defuse the tension and make a DC 13 Charisma (Persuasion) check. A character who offers the boss an impressive birthday present—such as 10 GP, a magic item, or one of the gifts from area D3—automatically succeeds on this check.

On a successful check, the goblins' attitudes improve to [Indifferent](variantrules.html#indifferent%20%5battitude%5d_xphb), and the boss invites the characters to join her birthday celebration. On a failed check, the goblins fight the party crashers.

Treasure. Stashed under the throne's ragged cushion are a fine tapestry sewn with silver and gold threads and one magic item; roll once on the [Magic Items](#hotb,4,magic%20items,0) table in [appendix A](#hotb,4,appendix%20a%3a%20equipment%20and%20magic%20items,0) (rerolling duplicates) to determine the item. The tapestry belongs to Vinx and is worth 90 GP.

If the characters give the tapestry to Vinx, she rewards them with the magic item from [area D2](#hotb,1,d2%3a%20dead-end%20tunnel,0). However, if she learns the characters intend to keep the tapestry, she hotly demands they return the family heirloom and fights them if they refuse.

### D5: Webbed Cavern

Webs drape the western half of this musky cave.

A [Giant Spider](bestiary.html#giant%20spider_xmm) lurks in a tunnel to the south. The [Hostile](variantrules.html#hostile%20%5battitude%5d_xphb) spider ambushes all non-goblins in its den.

Webs. The webs are [Difficult Terrain](variantrules.html#difficult%20terrain_xphb), but they aren't strong enough to restrain anyone. Each 10-foot-cube of webs has AC 10; HP 5; Vulnerability to Fire damage; and Immunity to Bludgeoning, Piercing, Poison, and Psychic damage.
