#### Doors

The doors are made of magical obsidian and have Immunity to all damage. They are unlocked, though the exterior doors are well defended (see areas [V2](../chapter.md#V2MiddleTier) and [V3](../chapter.md#V3TowerApex)). See “Doors” in the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#Doors).
