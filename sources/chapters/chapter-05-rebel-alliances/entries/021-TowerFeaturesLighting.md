#### Lighting

Interior areas ([V4](../chapter.md#V4Lounge)–[V5](../chapter.md#V5AEBedrooms)) are bathed in [Bright Light](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#BrightLight), thanks to [Continual Flame](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619065-continual-flame) spells cast on artistic sconces in each chamber.
