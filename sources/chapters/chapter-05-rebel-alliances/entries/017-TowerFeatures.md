### Tower Features

The Veiled Tower, which stands three hundred feet high, is made of magically treated obsidian that has Immunity to all damage. The tower has the following features.

<a id="TowerFeaturesCeilings"></a>


#### Ceilings

The ceilings in interior areas ([V4](../chapter.md#V4Lounge)–[V5](../chapter.md#V5AEBedrooms)) are 60 feet high.

<a id="TowerFeaturesDoors"></a>


#### Doors

The doors are made of magical obsidian and have Immunity to all damage. They are unlocked, though the exterior doors are well defended (see areas [V2](../chapter.md#V2MiddleTier) and [V3](../chapter.md#V3TowerApex)). See “Doors” in the [Dungeon Master’s Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/dms-toolbox#Doors).

<a id="TowerFeaturesExteriorAreas"></a>


#### Exterior Areas

Areas [V1](../chapter.md#V1VisitorReception)–[V3](../chapter.md#V3TowerApex) lie outside the tower.

<a id="TowerFeaturesLighting"></a>


#### Lighting

Interior areas ([V4](../chapter.md#V4Lounge)–[V5](../chapter.md#V5AEBedrooms)) are bathed in [Bright Light](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#BrightLight), thanks to [Continual Flame](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619065-continual-flame) spells cast on artistic sconces in each chamber.

<a id="TowerFeaturesTreasure"></a>


#### Treasure

The Red Wizards’ bedrooms (areas [V5a–e](../chapter.md#V5AEBedrooms)) contain significant treasure. If the characters are caught stealing from any Red Wizard, the Red Wizards demand their treasures be returned immediately. If a character refuses, the Red Wizards fight the characters to the death.
