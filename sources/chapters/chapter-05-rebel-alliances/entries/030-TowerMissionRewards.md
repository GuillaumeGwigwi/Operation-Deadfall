### Mission Reward

If the characters’ mission is successful, each character earns 7,800 XP.

#### Development

If this is the first mission the characters have completed this chapter, they receive one magic item from the list in “[Mission Rewards](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/aud/chapter-3-operation-deadfall#OperatonDeadfallRewards)” in chapter 3. Remind the characters to tell Wrynn which item they’ve chosen as their reward.

If this is the second mission the characters have completed, they receive no reply if they try to contact Wrynn via their [Sending Stone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9180147-sending-stones). Any reward they request doesn’t arrive. As the characters learn in “[Enemy Ambush](../chapter.md#EnemyAmbush)” later in this chapter, Szass Tam has discovered Operation Deadfall. Wrynn is dead or scrambling to escape Szass Tam’s minions, as described in “[Wrynn’s Bespoke Vestments](../chapter.md#EnemyAmbush)” in chapter 6.
